About us

We Believe in the Power of Connection.

Forlink is a platform that bridges the gap between businesses seeking quality video editing services and freelance editors looking for meaningful opportunities.

Our mission

Professional editing, within reach.

To make professional video editing services more accessible by connecting businesses and creators with reliable freelance video editors through a simple and efficient marketplace.

Our vision

To become a trusted creative connection platform where businesses and talented video editors can collaborate, grow, and succeed together.

Our core values

What guides every Forlink connection

QualityWe value excellent creative output.
ReliabilityWe believe in meeting expectations and deadlines.
AccessibilityWe make creative opportunities easier to find.
CollaborationWe encourage meaningful partnerships.
GrowthWe support businesses and freelancers in reaching their potential.

The Forlink brand

One Team. One Goal. Forlink.

Our mark joins two links into one chain, the way a business and an editor join into one team. It is built from blue, white and black: clear, confident and easy to read.

Forlink was started by computing students who are also experienced video editors, so we have lived both sides of the problem: finding the right editor, and being found.
