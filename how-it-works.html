How it works

A Simpler Way to Find the Right Creative Talent.

Forlink is a reverse job marketplace: clients post the project and the budget, and editors come to them with proposals.

For clients and businesses

Find the editor who fits your project

  1. Create a project description.
  2. Specify your budget and requirements.
  3. Receive proposals from interested editors.
  4. Review editor profiles and portfolios.
  5. Select the right freelancer.
  6. Collaborate and complete your project.

For freelance video editors

Find projects and grow your opportunities

  1. Join the Forlink community.
  2. Showcase your skills and portfolio.
  3. Browse available projects.
  4. Submit proposals to potential clients.
  5. Connect with businesses.
  6. Build experience and grow your opportunities.
